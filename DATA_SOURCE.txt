DATA SOURCE AND LIMITATIONS

The four records in data/commute_scenarios.json are hand-assigned hypothetical
values reused from the author's Practice 07 visualization. They are not a
survey, travel log, official transport timetable, or measurements of any route.
No personal information or observations of people are included.

The supplied P50 and P90 values are assumed scenario summaries. The analysis
does not estimate percentiles from individual observations. There is no
underlying trip-level sample, sample size, date range, or geographic coverage.
P50 means a median and P90 a 90th percentile only within this assumed scenario.

P90 minus P50 is the extra time between the two supplied reference points.
It is not a standard deviation, confidence interval, probability of delay,
or complete measure of reliability. Two quantiles cannot reconstruct a
distribution or show how often either mode is quicker on the same journey.

NOT A UM TRANSPORT STUDY. The labels are illustrative and must not be read
as findings about University of Macau transport or real commuting choices.
The project demonstrates reproducible comparison and visualization, not a
real-world transport recommendation.

No external data were downloaded. No claim about an external dataset's
license is made. There is no restriction implied about any real operator.
